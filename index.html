      Video Map is made for Riyadh: the map opens on the city and keeps to it, and places are looked for in it.

      In the 3D city everything is drawn; no satellite photo is shown. The landmarks, bridges, metro stations, mosques and shop fronts are simplified models made for this app, not surveys; windows, shop floors, pavements, road markings, crossings, what stands on the roofs and the cars in the car parks are set out by rule to look right, and the colours of roofs and walls are worked out from the photo. A chain's colours are its own; no logos are shown. By night the street lamps, the lit windows and the lights on the landmarks are set out by rule too: they show how Riyadh looks after dark, not which lamp is on.
